Ubuntu United Health Network Terms & Privacy Notice

Effective date:

This is a demo policy for coursework purposes. It explains how we handle submitted form information, security practices, and your rights.

1) Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide in the registration form, including contact details, health-history selections, and symptom descriptions so we can process intake and respond to care requests.

2) How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you regarding your intake request.
  • To prepare care staff for your visit or follow-up.
  • To improve service quality and patient experience.

3) Data Security and Retention

We use administrative and technical safeguards to protect submitted data. Information is retained only as long as necessary for intake, care operations, and legal obligations.

4) Sharing and Privacy

We do not sell personal data. We may share limited information with authorized healthcare operations personnel or service providers only when needed for intake support.

5) Your Choices and Rights

You may request updates to your submitted information, ask questions about privacy practices, or request deletion where legally permitted.

6) Contact

For policy questions, contact: privacy@ubuntuuhn.example.
